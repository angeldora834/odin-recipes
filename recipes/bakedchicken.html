Baked Chicken Recipe

Description

This baked chicken recipe is a simple and flavorful dish that is perfect for any meal. The chicken is seasoned with herbs and spices, then baked to perfection, resulting in a juicy and tender main course.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a small bowl, mix together the garlic powder, paprika, salt, black pepper, thyme, and rosemary.
  3. Rub the chicken breasts with olive oil and then coat them evenly with the spice mixture.
  4. Place the seasoned chicken breasts on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) and the chicken is no longer pink in the center.
  6. Remove from the oven and let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving.
